# Lumenotepad 1.3.1

## Buttons that fit in

- In the notebooks strip, the buttons now match the + and the notebooks beside them, with a thin line
  separating them from your notebooks.
- Beside the notebook's name, the pages button matches the + and pencil buttons below it.
- The bubble that brings hidden panels back now looks like Lumenotepad's menus, frosted on a Mac, and
  a thin line separates the show-panel buttons from Home and Preferences.

## Fixed

- Hiding a panel right after showing the other one could leave the bubble invisible. It now always
  appears.

---

**Your notes** are never touched by installing or updating: `~/Library/Application Support/Lumenotepad`
on macOS, the `userdata` folder beside the executable on Windows.

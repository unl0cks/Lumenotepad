# Lumenotepad 1.4.0

## Pick a layout

Preferences → Layout now starts with four small pictures. Pick one and the window rearranges
itself straight away. Your notebooks, sections and pages stay exactly as they are; only the way you
get around them changes.

- **Classic** is the window you know: the notebooks rail, sections and pages.
- **One sidebar** puts everything in one column on the left. The notebook name at the top switches
  notebooks. Each section is a small heading with its pages underneath, and clicking a heading folds
  it away. **New page** and **New section** sit at the bottom.
- **Tabs** shows sections as tabs above the page, with the pages in a column on the right. The
  notebook switcher moves up next to the Home button.
- **Focus** has no side columns at all. Above the page, a path reads *Notebook › Section › Page*, and
  each part opens a menu to jump somewhere else, add a page or add a sub-page. The toolbar stays out
  of the way until you click into a text box.

In One sidebar, Tabs and Focus:

- The toolbar keeps the everyday tools. **More**, at its end, shows the rest.
- The page button in the title bar hides the side column, and the little bubble brings it back.
- Right-click menus, sub-pages and their thread lines work just as they do in Classic.
- Single mode (pages only, no sections) works in every layout.
- Settings that only mean something in Classic, such as button placement and the notebooks rail,
  hide themselves until you switch back.

## Preferences, sorted into tabs

Every page in Preferences now has a row of tabs under its title, and each tab holds one group of
settings, so a page never turns into one long list:

- **General**: Startup, Home screen, Deleting
- **Appearance**: Theme, Glass & shape, Motion
- **Layout**: Panels, Toolbar, Pages, Paper
- **Writing**: Typing, Text, Spacing, Lists, Colors
- **Data & tools**: Saving & backup, Import & export, Maintenance

Settings that were scattered now live with their neighbours:

- The Canvas page is gone. Its Pages and Paper settings are tabs on the Layout page.
- Bullet colors and numbered lists moved from their own page into Writing › Lists, and no longer ask
  you to unlock the advanced settings first.
- Saving settings sit with backups in Data & tools, and the gallery settings sit with the home screen
  in General.
- The Editor page is called **Writing** now, and quick highlight has its own heading next to the
  toolbar palettes.

Searching still looks through every page at once. Results are grouped under headings such as
*Writing › Lists*, so you can see where each setting lives, and clearing the search takes you back to
the page you were on.

---

**Your notes** are never touched by installing or updating: `~/Library/Application Support/Lumenotepad`
on macOS, the `userdata` folder beside the executable on Windows.

# Lumenotepad 1.3.2

## Sub-pages look like threads

Sub-pages used to sit a little further in than their page, and that was all. Now they hang off it:

- A thin line runs down from the page's title and curves into each of its sub-pages. Several
  sub-pages share one line, and the last one curves off it.
- A sub-page's own sub-pages get their own line, one step further in, while the outer line keeps
  running past them to the next sub-page.
- A selected sub-page's highlight starts where its line ends, instead of stretching underneath it.
- Folding a page away takes its lines with it, and the line joins straight on to the next sub-page.

---

**Your notes** are never touched by installing or updating: `~/Library/Application Support/Lumenotepad`
on macOS, the `userdata` folder beside the executable on Windows.
